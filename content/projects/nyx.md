NYX (formerly known as Cath) is the first and only Call of Duty: Mobile statistics and gunsmith bot, and also a fully featured multipurpose Discord bot. In addition to CODM-focused tools, NYX offers a wide range of features including moderation, music, and an in-server economy.
All Call of Duty statistics are powered by Stats on Duty and Round Table. Builds are curated by Team NYX.

## Call of Duty Mobile

- Weapon statistics provided by **Stats on Duty** and **Round Table**
- Curated Gunsmith Builds by various content creators like [Jokesta](https://www.youtube.com/Jokesta), [dGamerHitman](https://www.youtube.com/@dGamerHitman), [Path.exe](https://www.youtube.com/@path_exe), [Stats On Duty](https://discord.gg/nicecat) and more.
- Detailed perk breakdowns, including in-game effects and usage
- Comprehensive scorestreak information with preview videos

> We also used provide access to our internal database, which includes in-depth weapon statistics, gunsmith builds from multiple content creators, and other Call of Duty–related data.

## Metrics

- At its peak, NYX was active in **1,200+ Discord servers**.
- Served over **178,000 unique users**.
- Featured manually tested data for **104+ weapons**, provided by [Stats on Duty](https://discord.gg/nicecat) (Project Lighthouse).
- Hosted **900+ gunsmith builds** from collaborating content creators.
- Offered **50+ commands** spanning utilities, server management, and fun mini-games.
